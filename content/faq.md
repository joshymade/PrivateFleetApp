# FAQ

> Answers about PrivateFleet for drivers, Safety, and Admin.

## What is PrivateFleet?

PrivateFleet is a mobile-first app for a private truck fleet. Drivers log loads and pay-related details, capture trailer and tractor damage photos, and coordinate with Safety and Admin. Safety reviews damage and inbox referrals. Admin manages users, roles, and fleet settings.

## What can other users do with the app?

It depends on role:

- **Drivers** manage their own loads, damage uploads, and profile settings. They cannot see another driver’s private load or pay information.
- **Safety** reviews fleet damage photos and items drivers send to the Safety inbox. They mark reports as viewed and follow up on referrals.
- **Admin** manages users and roles, has broader operational visibility, and handles account or contact requests.

Shared fleet damage (trailer/tractor photos and related metadata) exists so the fleet can track equipment condition. Personal load and earnings data stay with the owning driver unless your fleet’s policies say otherwise.

## How private is my personal load information?

Load numbers, routes, stops, mileage, earnings, off days, week preferences, and similar pay/load records are private to the driver who owns them. Other drivers cannot open or browse your loads. Use Account settings (for example hide-money controls, where available) to reduce on-screen exposure of pay amounts on your own device.

## Does Safety have access to data I did not submit to them?

Safety’s day-to-day work is damage review and the Safety inbox. Damage photos and metadata that are part of the fleet damage workflow can be visible to Safety according to role. Drivers send specific reports to the Safety inbox when they want Safety attention.

Safety does **not** get a general window into your private load book, pay entries, or other personal load details that were never shared through damage/referral flows. If something was not submitted to Safety (and is not part of shared damage review), treat it as outside Safety’s inbox access. For edge cases or fleet policy questions, contact Admin from Account → Contact.

## Who can see damage photos?

Damage photos are stored so the fleet can review trailer and tractor condition. Drivers upload work-related photos; Safety and Admin can review according to their role. Sending a report to the Safety inbox is separate from Safety marking a report as viewed.

## How do I get help or request changes?

Use **Account → Contact** to reach Admin about account info, locked profile fields, bugs, or ideas. Legal drafts live under **Account → Legal** (Privacy, Terms, and this FAQ).
